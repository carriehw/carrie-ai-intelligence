Key Highlights重點摘要
  • Meta expanded its AI-content disclosure framework across Facebook and Instagram ads this month: creative using AI-generated or AI-significantly-edited visuals, voiceovers or video must carry a label in Ads Manager at submission, shown to users in the "About this ad" panel.Meta 本月擴大了 Facebook 及 Instagram 廣告的 AI 內容披露機制：凡使用 AI 生成或經 AI 大幅修改的視覺元素、配音或影片，均須在 Ads Manager 提交時附上標籤，並在「關於此廣告」欄目向用戶顯示。
  • Detection combines Meta's own AI-content-detection models with industry-standard C2PA metadata checks for content made in third-party tools such as Midjourney or DALL-E — undisclosed AI content is now an active reason for ad rejection, and Meta says it already accounts for an estimated 14% of all ad rejections in 2026.偵測方式結合 Meta 自家的 AI 內容偵測模型，以及業界標準的 C2PA 元數據檢查，用以識別在 Midjourney、DALL-E 等第三方工具製作的內容——未披露的 AI 內容現時是廣告被拒登的其中一個主動理由，Meta 表示這已佔 2026 年全部廣告拒登個案約 14%。
  • The requirement runs alongside mainland China's own mandatory AI-content labelling rules and platform-level rules on Xiaohongshu and Douyin — advertisers running the same creative across both ecosystems now face two separate, not-identical labelling regimes.這項要求與中國內地強制性 AI 內容標識規定，以及小紅書、抖音等平台層面的規則同步存在——在兩個生態系統投放同一套素材的廣告主，現時要應付兩套不盡相同的標籤制度。
Industry Insight行業洞察

Two separate regulatory tracks — Meta's platform policy in the West, government-mandated labelling in mainland China — are converging on the same requirement from different directions: disclose AI-generated ad content or risk it being pulled. One compliance checklist covering both, rather than one built for whichever market a campaign launched in first, avoids rebuilding creative twice. 兩條不同的監管路徑——Meta 在西方的平台政策，與內地由政府主導的強制標識規定——正從不同方向走向同一個要求：AI 生成的廣告內容要標示，否則有機會被下架。與其為最先出稿的市場度身訂造一份合規清單，不如做一份同時覆蓋兩邊要求的清單，就不必日後為另一個市場重做一次創意。
